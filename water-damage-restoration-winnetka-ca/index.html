When water is spreading in Winnetka

Winnetka sits directly west of Reseda. Corbin Avenue is the shared edge of the two neighborhood councils, so it is a short drive from our Saticoy Street base. Dispatch is staffed around the clock. When you call, say your nearest cross street and whether the water is clean supply water or came up from a drain, because that decides the water category and the equipment.

For any Winnetka job, you can ask the crew whether its drying plan follows ANSI/IICRC S500, the published standard for water damage restoration.

If Water Is Still Spreading

Winnetka lots run deep, with garages, back houses and long yard runs. Work through these before the crew pulls up.

Run the meter test

With every tap and sprinkler off, watch the meter's leak-detection gauge, which is the LADWP test for a leak. If it does, water is escaping somewhere, possibly underground or in the irrigation.

Shut the main and the sprinklers

Close the house main, then turn off the irrigation controller so a timer does not restart the flow. Check back houses and garage sinks, which often have their own valves.

Power and footing

Skip the breaker panel if the garage floor is wet. If you can reach it from dry ground, switch off circuits serving wet rooms. Anything beyond that is a call to the utility or 911.

Pull the valuables and call

Lift boxes off garage floors, move rugs and electronics up, keep pets and kids away from drain water and call the 24/7 line.

History

The Weeks Poultry Colony and Its Big Parcels

In 1922, Charles Weeks founded a poultry colony on 600 acres here. It was divided into one-acre parcels, each with a house, a poultry shed and a feed garden, and about 500 families lived there at its peak. City surveyors found seven surviving colony houses, some still with chicken coops, and CSUN's Weeks collection says many Winnetka houses still sit on large parcels.

For water damage, a big parcel means detached garages, back houses and a long line from the meter to the house. A leak in any of those can soak floors and wall bases without reaching the main house. Check outbuildings after a leak. If old outbuilding framing or flooring is soaked, water damage cleanup removes the ruined material before drying.

Flood check

The LA River Wash, a County Channel Nearby and Your Own Map

The LA River Wash is the south boundary of the neighborhood council area, and Victory Boulevard runs beside it. Being near a flood channel does not place a house in a flood zone, and we will not guess. Run your address through FEMA's map search and the LA County Flood Zone Determination tool instead.

In neighboring Canoga Park, LA County says the channel at the Bell Creek and Arroyo Calabasas confluence is sized for a 50-year storm. That is a reminder that channels have limits. Flood losses are commonly excluded from homeowners and renters policies, so ask your insurer whether a separate flood policy is in place before the wet season. For storm water inside a home, our crews run flood damage restoration jobs.

Corridors

Winnetka Avenue, Sherman Way and Newer Apartments

City surveyors call Winnetka Avenue one of the major north-south arteries of the area, and the Winnetka Recreation Center sits at 8401 Winnetka Avenue. Winnetka Palms, an affordable housing project on Sherman Way near Winnetka, topped out in 2022. Newer multi-unit buildings usually stack bathrooms and kitchens, so a supply-line leak can wet more than one unit.

If you rent in a building like that, report the leak to management in writing and keep photos with dates. We log moisture readings by room, which gives the property manager and your insurer a clear record of what got wet and when it dried.

Rebuild

After the Water Is Out

Extraction is the first step, not the last. Wet drywall, baseboards and insulation are dried or cut out, and then the finished surfaces are replaced. Water damage repair covers that rebuild. LADBS says some repairs qualify for an Express Permit and others do not, so ask LADBS before closing up walls.
