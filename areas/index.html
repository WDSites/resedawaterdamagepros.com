How Dispatch Works

One Base in Reseda, Five Neighbors Around It

Every call to the 24/7 line is answered by the same dispatch, whether the water is in Reseda or a few neighborhoods away. The dispatcher asks for the address, the source of the water, whether power is safe and what has been done so far, then arranges a licensed and insured crew. Same-day response when available.

Each neighboring town has its own page with local detail, ZIP codes and the services that matter most there. Use them to see how the dispatch handles your side of the Valley.

North and West

Northridge, Winnetka and Woodland Hills

Reseda sits at the center of these three. Northridge lies to the north, with ZIP codes 91324, 91325 and 91330 among its most common. Give the dispatcher your cross streets and which ZIP you are in, since Northridge spans several.

Winnetka is directly west, and its eastern edge at Corbin Avenue meets Reseda's western edge. Its main ZIP is 91306. Further southwest, Woodland Hills uses 91364 and 91367.

South and East

Tarzana and Encino

South of Victory Boulevard, Tarzana shares a border with Reseda and uses ZIP 91356. Further east, Encino uses 91316 and 91436, and a call from there is a longer run than one inside Reseda, so the dispatcher will talk through timing honestly.

Also Nearby

A Note on Canoga Park

Canoga Park lies just west of Winnetka and is also served, though it does not have its own page on this site. Its ZIP codes 91303 and 91304 are nearby, and a call from there is handled the same way as any other. If you are not sure whether your address is covered, call and ask.

Not seeing your neighborhood here is not a reason to hold off during an emergency. The services we offer are the same everywhere we dispatch, and the dispatcher can confirm coverage during the call.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you serve my neighborhood if it is not listed?

Possibly. Call the 24/7 line with your address and the dispatcher will confirm whether a crew can reach you. Canoga Park, for example, is served without a page of its own.

Is the service the same in every town?

Yes. The same six services and the same dispatch apply across all five communities. Local pages differ in detail such as ZIP codes and common building types.

Do I need to know my ZIP code when I call?

Your street address is more useful, along with the nearest cross street. The ZIP simply helps the dispatcher confirm which community you are in.
