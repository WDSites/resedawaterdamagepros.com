Canoga Park in the San Fernando Valley Flats

A separate City Planning page on the Southwest Valley Plans describes it as in the San Fernando Valley flats and places Reseda in a different plan area. Sherman Way, Owensmouth Avenue and the Older Commercial Core

The Community Plan Land Use chapter refers to the historic commercial core of Canoga Park along Sherman Way and calls for preserving pedestrian orientation along Sherman Way and Owensmouth Avenue. Blocks like these can mix shops and apartments under shared roofs or walls.

Emergency

If Water Is Spreading Right Now

Stay out of water that could carry current or contamination, and keep pets out too. Sewage and other dirty water call for trained handling. A box fan or a shop vacuum will not make that safe.

Before Help Arrives

Stop at any step that looks unsafe.

Water valve

Close the fixture valve or main only if you can reach it without wading. In an apartment building, tell the manager.

Electricity

If your feet are in water, leave breakers and electrical equipment alone. If you see immediate danger, call the utility or 911.

Photos

When safe, photograph walls, floors, ceilings and belongings before moving anything.

The call

Call (818) 337-2642 and say where the water started and what it looks like.

Creeks

Bell Creek, Arroyo Calabasas and the Headwaters Project

Los Angeles County Public Works describes Canoga Park as located at the confluence of Bell Creek and Arroyo Calabasas. Its Headwaters Area Project page says the area faces hydraulic challenges related to flood risk reduction. That is county flood-control language and does not document flooding in any home.

A Winnetka Neighborhood Council news page places the LA River Headwaters project in the Canoga Park area, with work along Bell Creek and Calabasas Creek between Jordan and Owensmouth Avenues. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. For your own block, enter the address into FEMA's Flood Map Service Center.

Drying

Extraction, Moisture Readings and Drying

First comes pumping or extraction of standing water. Moisture meters then trace how far it reached into drywall, framing and flooring. Air movers and dehumidifiers pull out what remains, and meter checks continue until the materials read dry. The published reference standard for water damage restoration is ANSI/IICRC S500.

Drain overflows fall under sewage backup cleanup, rain entering a building under storm water damage, and wet wall cavities under structural drying.

Housing

What the Census Reporter Profiles Show

A Census Reporter profile of ZIP Code Tabulation Area 91303 lists a population of 29,805 and 11,405 housing units. The profile for 91304 lists 52,474 people and 18,807 housing units. A ZCTA is a statistical area and not the neighborhood itself.

ZIP 91304 is shared with West Hills, so state your street address at the start of the call. The profiles omit renter share and year built, so this page claims nothing about either. Water mitigation begins with finding where the water went.

Insurance

Documenting a Loss for Your Insurer

Save your photos, moisture readings and an itemized scope. Read your policy and ask your insurer what it applies to, since coverage turns on the cause and policy terms, and flood usually needs its own coverage. Near Lanark Recreation Center at 21816 Lanark Street or anywhere in Canoga Park, call (818) 337-2642 when you find water.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Which ZIP codes does this page cover?

ZIPs 91303 and 91304, with 91304 as primary. It is shared with West Hills, so give your address on the call.

Does the county creek work mean my home will flood?

No. The county page describes flood risk reduction work on channels and does not say homes flood. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified.

Can you help with a shop or apartment building?

In general terms, yes: extraction, drying and moisture readings in each affected unit. Photograph every unit and tell the property manager.

Will fans alone dry a wet wall?

Usually not. Water can sit in wall cavities and subfloors, and readings show whether materials are dry.
