Northridge sits just north of Reseda. The City's community plan describes the area as bounded by the Ronald Reagan Freeway (SR-118) and Devonshire Street on the north, Bull Creek on the east, Roscoe Boulevard on the south and Tampa Avenue on the west. Emergency dispatch runs around the clock, and naming the nearest cross street from that grid helps the dispatcher place you before they ask for the full address.

The Northridge plan treats the Reseda Boulevard business district as the community's focal point. When water is spreading in Northridge

A pipe does not wait for a weekday. Whether it is 3 a.m. or a holiday, the line is staffed, and a crew is sent as soon as the dispatcher has your address and the water category. Same-day response when available.

If Water Is Still Spreading

Northridge has long driveways and a lot of shared walls. These four moves protect both while the crew is on the way.

Close the right valve

In a house, shut the main valve near the street side or on an exterior wall. In an apartment or condo, close the fixture valve under the sink or behind the toilet and ask the manager about the building shutoff.

Cut power with care

If water reached outlets or the panel area, switch off breakers only when the panel is dry and you can reach it on dry ground. Otherwise stay back and call the utility or 911 before you call us.

Lift and photograph

Raise furniture onto foil or blocks and move rugs and electronics up. Photograph the water line and the source first. Renters should send those photos to the landlord the same day.

Warn the units below

Knock on the door under yours. Keep children and pets out of water that touched a toilet, a drain or a floor, then call the 24/7 line.

Drainage

Washes, Channels and Checking Your Own Address

The plan names Bull Creek, a Los Angeles County flood control channel, along with Wilbur Wash and Aliso Canyon Wash as drainage corridors. It also lists floods and fires among the disasters that have affected the community. None of that puts your street in a flood zone. Search your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center and read the result yourself.

Homeowners and renters policies commonly exclude flood, so ask your insurer about separate coverage. LA County storm guidance adds that a 30-day wait before flood coverage starts is typical, so the time to ask is before the rain. After a storm, flood damage restoration covers Category 2 and 3 water.

History

The 1994 Earthquake and Repairs Behind Your Walls

The magnitude 6.7 earthquake of January 17, 1994 took this community's name, although Council District 12 places the epicenter near Reseda Boulevard and Saticoy Street, south of Northridge proper. For a buyer, the practical point is paperwork. Ask LADBS what permits are on file for an older address and ask the seller which plumbing was replaced, because a hidden repair is where a slow leak hides.

When a slow leak has been wetting drywall for weeks, water damage repair comes after drying, and mold may already be present. Mold needs moisture, so the leak must be fixed first. Testing and removal follow ANSI/IICRC S520, and any health question belongs to a physician. Our mold remediation team can scope the work.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is emergency water removal available in Northridge?

Yes. Same-day response when available, including nights, weekends and holidays. Extraction units, truck-mounted or hand-carried, go out first, with air movers and LGR dehumidifiers for drying afterward. Stop the source, then call the 24/7 line.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Northridge?

Yes: 91324, 91325 and 91330. ZIP 91324 includes the Tampa Avenue corridor and Northridge Fashion Center. ZIP 91325 is the largest and includes Sherwood Forest. ZIP 91330 is the single-entity code for the CSUN campus at 18111 Nordhoff Street.

The ceiling near CSUN is leaking from the unit above. What do I do?

Stop the water if you can reach a fixture valve, photograph the damage, and tell your landlord in writing. Then call us so the wet drywall is dried before mold can start. For unresolved unsafe conditions at a multifamily rental, the Los Angeles Housing Department takes code violation reports.

Is my Northridge address in a flood zone?

We cannot tell you that from here. Search the address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center or use the LA County Flood Zone Determination site. Standard homeowners policies commonly exclude flood, so check your policy.

Who do I call for a broken water main or a meter leak?

LADWP lists a 24/7 line for water emergencies at 1-800-342-5397. Leaks on your side of the meter are yours to stop and dry, and that is where we come in.
