About These Guides

Written for the Person With the Problem

These guides are written for Reseda homeowners and renters who are standing in front of a stain, a soft spot in a floor or a damp smell and want to know what it means. They explain what to check, what can wait and what should not wait, in the order you would actually need it.

Each guide sticks to one question. You will find ceiling leaks and drywall repair, wood floors that cup or buckle, burst pipes and slab leaks, insurance claim basics for California, and the mold topics callers raise most often, including mold on ceilings and walls and how to choose a specialist.

The posts are grouped by problem type: repair, leaks, drying, mold and general guidance. If you are not sure where your situation fits, start with the group closest to what you can see. A stain on a ceiling points to the repair and leak guides. A musty closet points to the mold guides.

Every guide ends with a short Reseda note that sticks to checkable facts from public agencies, and every guide says plainly when a job is beyond a do-it-yourself fix. They are general information, not professional, insurance or legal advice, and your policy and your lease always come first.

If water is moving right now, skip the reading. Shut off the source if you can do so safely and call the 24/7 line.
