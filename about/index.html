Who We Are

A Dispatch for Water Damage and Mold Restoration

Reseda Water Damage Pros is a water damage and mold restoration dispatch serving Reseda, California and nearby neighborhoods. When you call, you reach a person who asks what happened, where the water is coming from and whether anyone is in danger, then arranges a licensed and insured restoration professional for the job.

We are based at 19301-19371 Saticoy St in Reseda, 91335. The business is call-only by design. Water losses do not wait for a message to be read, and a phone call is the fastest way to decide what happens in the next hour.

The line is open 24 hours a day, seven days a week, including holidays. A burst pipe at night or a washing machine hose that fails on a Sunday gets the same attention as a Tuesday morning call.

What We Do

Six Services, Matched to the Stage of the Loss

The work covers the full sequence of a water loss. Emergency water removal clears standing water, water mitigation controls the first 24 to 72 hours, and flood damage restoration handles storm and sewage losses. After that come water damage cleanup and water damage repair.

Mold remediation is the secondary service. It exists because moisture that is not dried in time can lead to mold, and because callers regularly ask for it after a leak. Our services overview explains which one fits which problem.

Standards

How the Work Is Meant to Be Done

Two published industry standards guide this work. ANSI/IICRC S500 covers professional water damage restoration, including water categories, classes of loss and drying goals. ANSI/IICRC S520 covers professional mold remediation, including containment and verification.

We cannot tell you what another company does, but you can ask any restoration company you call whether its crews work to S500 and S520, and ask to see how they document drying. A good answer includes moisture readings, a drying log and a written scope.

You can also verify any California contractor's license on the state CSLB website. The CSLB advises against paying large amounts upfront and recommends a written contract, and that is sound advice no matter which company you hire.

Pricing

Scope and Price Explained Before Work Starts

Most people calling about water damage want a number, and they should get one they can trust. That number comes after the loss has been looked at, because price follows the water category, the size of the area, the materials involved and how long drying will take.

Before anything is torn out or any equipment is set, you are told what the plan is, why it is needed and how the price is built. If something changes during drying, such as hidden wet insulation behind a wall, the change is explained before it is done.

What We Do Not Do

Honest Limits

We do not do waterproofing, and we do not rent drying equipment. Restoration dries and repairs after water arrives. A foundation that seeps every winter needs a waterproofing contractor, and a wet wall cavity needs someone who can read moisture meters and take responsibility for the result.

We also do not decide your insurance claim, promise that a claim will be paid or give medical advice about mold. Your insurer decides coverage, and health questions belong with a physician. What we can do is document the loss clearly, with photos, moisture readings and a drying log, so the paperwork supports you.
