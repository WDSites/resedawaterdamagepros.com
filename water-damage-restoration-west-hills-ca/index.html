The Neighborhood

West Hills at the Western Edge of the Valley

The Council District 12 community page places West Hills in the western San Fernando Valley, covering 8.53 square miles. It names Chatsworth to the north, Woodland Hills to the south and Canoga Park to the east. To the west lie the Upper Las Virgenes Canyon Open Space Preserve and Bell Canyon.

The West Hills Neighborhood Council map traces its boundary along Nordhoff Street, the city limits, Victory Boulevard, Shoup Avenue, Roscoe Boulevard and Topanga Canyon Boulevard. Our water damage restoration line answers calls from this part of the Valley, and the first thing we ask is your address.

History and Water

Ranch Roots and Creek Headwaters

The council district page says the area began as Owensmouth in 1912, when George Platt bought the land and started a dairy farm. It later became Canoga Park and, in 1987, West Hills. The same page says Bell Creek and Dayton Creek are headwaters of the Los Angeles River.

That history is context, not a damage forecast. The council district page says Orcutt Ranch Estate and Shadow Ranch Park are City Historic-Cultural Monuments, so tell any contractor early if your property is protected.

First Response

What to Do Before Help Arrives

If Water Is Still Spreading

Five steps to take in the first minutes, in this order.

Keep people and pets clear

Stay out of rooms where water reaches outlets, cords or appliances. Water that has touched electrical equipment can be hazardous.

Shut off the water only if safe

Close the main or the fixture valve only when you can get to it without stepping into water. If your feet are wet, stay away from the panel and every plug.

Photograph the damage

When it is safe, photograph each room, the water source and damaged belongings from wide and close angles before moving anything.

Call for immediate danger

Call 911 or the utility for sparks, gas smells or a ceiling that sags. Sewage or other contaminated water needs professionals. Household fans and ordinary vacuums cannot handle it.

Call and give your address

Drying

Extraction, Moisture Readings and Drying

The first job is pulling out standing water using pumps or extraction units. Moisture meters then map what the water reached, including walls and subfloors you cannot see. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the structure until readings reach the dry standard.

The published reference standard here is ANSI/IICRC S500. Read our structural drying page for how a drying log works, and our water mitigation page for the steps that stop damage from spreading.

Insurance

Documenting a Claim in General Terms

Save your photos, the drying readings and a line-by-line scope in one folder. Next, review your policy and ask the insurer how it treats this kind of loss. Coverage turns on how the water got in and what your policy says, and flood usually needs its own policy.
