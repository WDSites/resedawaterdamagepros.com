The Neighborhood

What Lake Balboa Is

The Los Angeles City Council District 6 page calls Lake Balboa the newest community in the district. It was once part of Van Nuys and became its own community in 2007. The page describes it as predominantly residential and near the 405 and 101, with the Balboa Sports Complex, Hjelte Sports Complex, Woodley Lakes Golf Course and Japanese Garden as local anchors.

ZIP Codes

Which ZIP Code Applies to Your Address

City Recreation and Parks lists Lake Balboa Park, also called Anthony C. Beilenson Park, at 6300 Balboa Boulevard in ZIP 91406, so that is the ZIP on this page. The Lake Balboa Neighborhood Council says some blocks near Roscoe Boulevard and Louise Avenue stay in Northridge ZIP 91325. ZIP coverage of the whole neighborhood is not verified, so tell the dispatcher your street address when you call.

Pipe Failures

When a Water Main or Supply Line Fails

An NBC Los Angeles report describes a broken 20-inch water main on the 16400 block of Vanowen Street on February 1, 2011. It flooded apartments, homes and businesses and displaced about 60 people. That was a pipe failure, not river flooding.

Smaller failures start inside the house, such as a supply line under a sink or a washing machine hose. See appliance leak water damage for how those spread, and water mitigation for the process from first call to dry-out.

Rain and the Basin

Sepulveda Basin, Rain Closures and Your Home

Recreation and Parks says Lake Balboa Park lies within the Sepulveda Basin Recreation Area and may close in rainy weather. An Army Corps of Engineers story calls Sepulveda Dam flood risk management infrastructure. That describes the dam, not any home.

A Lake Balboa Neighborhood Council notice dated April 13, 2012 lists road and park access closures after heavy rain with no home flooding stated. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Search your address at the FEMA Flood Map Service Center. If rain reaches your door, see storm water damage.

First Response

What To Do Before Help Arrives

Sewage and contaminated water are professional jobs. Household fans and an ordinary vacuum are not enough. See sewage backup cleanup first.

If Water Is Still Spreading

Five steps for the first minutes.

Stay clear of hazards

Water around outlets, cords or a panel may be electrically hazardous, and drain or toilet water may be contaminated. Keep people and pets out.

Water off, if reachable

Close the fixture valve or the main only when that is safe. For a failed main in the street, call the utility, or 911 if there is immediate danger.

Do not touch breakers wet

Never handle breakers or electrical equipment while standing in water.

Photograph the damage

When safe, photograph the source, each wet room and affected belongings.

Drying

How Extraction and Drying Work

A crew pumps out standing water first, then measures moisture in floors and wall bases to learn how far it spread. Air movers and dehumidifiers do the drying, and readings repeat until materials test dry. The published reference standard for this work is ANSI/IICRC S500. Details are on structural drying.

Insurance

Documentation for an Insurance Claim

Before any claim call, collect photos, readings and an itemized scope. Your policy and your insurer decide how the cause of loss is treated. Flood is usually its own coverage.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What ZIP code is Lake Balboa?

The park address is ZIP 91406. Some blocks near Roscoe Boulevard and Louise Avenue stay in Northridge ZIP 91325. Whole-neighborhood coverage is not verified, so give your address.

Did the Sepulveda Basin flood Lake Balboa homes?

We found no source saying so. A 2012 notice lists road and park closures, and the home flooding we found, in 2011, came from a broken water main.

What should I do first after a pipe bursts?

Shut the water off only if you can reach the valve safely, keep people and pets away from electrical hazards, photograph the damage and call.

How is a wet wall or floor dried?

Standing water is extracted, then moisture readings guide air movers and dehumidifiers until materials read dry.

Is flood damage covered by insurance?

That depends on the cause and your policy. Flood is usually its own coverage, so ask your insurer.
